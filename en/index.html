How to start

Three steps and your hands are already busy.

  1. Ask and stay updated

    No form to fill in before you come. For details, email us or message the admins of the WhatsApp group.

  2. Come for an intro

    A tour of the space, a safety briefing and a small first project.

  3. Create regularly

    Drop in during open hours or sign up for a workshop.

    Frequently asked questions

    Everything about coming, signing up and what to do.

    Do I need to register first?

    No. Just come during opening hours, no form needed. (The form is only for signing up to sewing.) For questions, email us or message one of the admins of the quiet WhatsApp group.

    When are you open?

    Mondays and Tuesdays, 17:00–21:00. The library closes at 19:00. After that, knock on the entrance door and wait for a volunteer to open it.

    Where are you?

    1 Kronenberg St., Rehovot, the new Rehovot library, second floor.

    Is there parking?

    Yes, the parking lot in front of the library is free.

    Who can come?

    Everyone. Children under 13 come with a parent or accompanying adult, who takes an active part and joins the child in the activity. The space is a place for creating together, not a class or a childcare service. From age 13 you can come on your own.

    Does it cost anything?

    Entry is free, and for now small prints are free too.

    Do I need experience, and what can I try?

    No experience needed. You can try electronics, 3D modeling and printing, programming and AI, and get guidance and advice on personal projects. Equipment is used with a volunteer's guidance.

    Can I bring my own model or materials?

    You can bring your own models, and the crew will help make sure the design is ready to print. For safety reasons, only the space's own materials are used.

    Can we come as a small group?

    Yes. Small groups can coordinate in advance and come together, for workshops as well. To arrange it, email us or message one of the admins of the quiet WhatsApp group.

    How do I sign up for sewing?

    Fill in the sign-up form and we'll get back to you. You can also email us or message one of the admins of the quiet WhatsApp group.

    How do I hear about workshops and changes?

    In the calendar on this page and in the quiet WhatsApp group.

    Visit the space

    Just drop in during opening hours. For questions, email us or reach out to one of the admins in the WhatsApp group.
